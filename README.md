
The mod adds the following systems and additions to trade:
- DataPack driving
- Rework some villagers trade offers
- Rework wandering trader offers 
- Consistency System
- Variety System
- Leveling System [Will be in future updates]
- Endless Trading System [Will be in future updates]
- Trade config commands
- JEI integration [Will be in future updates]

## Consistency system
Some trades continue the previous ones. For example, if a mason was selling red terracotta, then in the next trade he will be able to sell only red glazed terracotta. Enchantment books at all levels of the librarian will have the same type of enchantment, but different levels depending on the seller's level. Books intended for equipment will now be sold by equipment vendors. At the first levels, only equipment with enchantment, on the last level only a book with the same enchantment.

## Variety System
Villagers of different biomes will have one or more unique biome trade offers. ==Basically, these will be decorative blocks or various objects. as well as a small number of enchantment books.== The scatter of trading is useful for balancing tradehalls - one player will not have time to gather all the villagers with all the books until someone else appears on this market.

## Leveling system [Will be in future updates]
The leveling of villagers will be carried out in two ways: classic and quest. The classic way will become ineffective and will need to spend more time and resources on the villager. The quest method is to conclude quest deals. Such offers appear once per level and disappear with the villager going to the next level. In this deal, the vendor asks for unusual and rare items and offers unique items for them with unique properties.

## Endless trading system [Will be in future updates]
This system is made to replace the Voidtrade. When making deals of the expert level, the villager will restore all offers.


## Commands
`/Merchant Edit <MerchantEntity>` is the main command for managing the vendor. Accepts only creatures like `MerchantEntity`, that is, the Villagers and the Wandering Trader.

`... addid <id>` - adds a new trade on his ID. All trade functions are calculated.

`... remove <index>` - removes the trade from the list of the merchant by its number. The count starts with 0.
